Privacy Policy: gta6-clipper

Last updated: 12 November 2026

gta6-clipper is a private tool used only by the owner of the GTAuploads channels. It has no other users.

gta6-clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the Google Privacy Policy, which applies to data accessed through YouTube API Services.

Data we access

  • TikTok: the owner's own basic profile information (username, display name) to confirm the correct account, and permission to upload videos to that account.
  • YouTube: the owner's own channel ID and title to confirm the correct channel, and permission to upload videos to it.
  • YouTube statistics of the owner's own videos (views, watch time, likes/dislikes) and the public comments posted on them, used only to summarise what content performs well for the owner. Comments are not stored with commenters' names beyond 30 days and are never shared.
  • Instagram (when connected): the owner's own professional account, to publish videos and read their statistics.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent anywhere except TikTok and Google when uploading.

Data of other people

The app does not collect personal data of viewers or other users.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google/YouTube at Google security settings, and from TikTok or Instagram account settings. Local tokens can be deleted by the owner, and stored YouTube data is deleted within 7 days after access is revoked.

Cookies

The app is a command-line tool and does not use cookies or store information on any visitor's device.
