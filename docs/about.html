How it works

GTAuploads is run by one person. Behind it is a private tool, gta6-clipper, that helps make and publish the clips. It has no other users.

From stream to short

  1. Source. Public recordings of GTA 6 streams from creators who allow clipping (see Creators).
  2. Selection. The stream is transcribed and the strongest moments are chosen.
  3. Editing. Each moment becomes a vertical 9:16 video with Spanish subtitles, a short on-screen hook and a credit line naming the streamer.
  4. Review. Every finished video is checked before it is scheduled.
  5. Publishing. Videos are posted to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

How our app uses TikTok

The app connects only to the GTAuploads TikTok account (@gtauploadsx), using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. It uses this access to:

  • upload our own edited videos to our account, as drafts or posts;
  • read basic account information, to confirm it is posting to the right account.

It never posts to anyone else's account and never collects data about other TikTok users. Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

How our app uses YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos on our own channel and to read their view counts. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
