# Playbook: what goes viral on this channel

Written by the analyst (agent 5) from real view counts and read by the selector (agent 2) before every stream. Anything here overrides the selector's defaults.

## Data status (read first)
- 8 shorts so far, all IlloJuan: 3–27 views each, median 16.5, 0 comments. This is cold-start noise.
- Nothing below is "confirmed" yet. A pattern needs at least 3 clips at 2x the median or better before it moves up.
- Predicted viral chance has not tracked views (rank correlation ≈ −0.15). #2 was predicted 80% and got 8 views.

## Owner rules (always respect these)
- No slow talking-head clips without a payoff. Clip #1 (IlloJuan talking before GTA 5) was removed for that reason.

## What gets views (early signals, not confirmed)
- Gameplay that shows a plan going wrong. #7 brag then AI ruins the heist (27, best), #4 cheating then terrace ripped off (19 views, 5 likes), #8 forgot the bike (19). Gameplay clips average about 17 views; clips without gameplay average about 10.
- The first line says what just happened. #7 opens "Acaba de atropellar a una mujer", #4 opens "Mira, atento. Así se solucionan unos cuernos". #2 opens with "Tranquilos que se puede, eh..." and got 8.
- Curiosity-gap hooks that don't give away the ending: "Camino al atraco... y pasa esto" (#7), "Todos cogen la bici menos él..." (#8).
- Relatable setups plus physical destruction get engagement. #4 has 5 likes on 19 views; every other clip has 0–1 likes.
- Non-gameplay clips only work with a personal payoff. #9's live countdown that lands on "8 days left" (19) worked; GTA 6 news by itself did not (#6, #10).

## Avoid
- Webpage, menu or facecam-only clips built on one funny line (#10: 3 views, lowest).
- "Look at this thing someone else made" reveals with no stakes for the streamer (#6: 7).
- Hooks that only label the topic ("Las normas del festival de GTA 6", #10).
- Warm-up lines before the action. Cut straight to the event (#2).

## Hypotheses (status)
- DROPPED: "Visual fails beat funny lines" as stated. The visual fail #2 got 8. The real split seems to be gameplay with stakes vs. no gameplay.
- DROPPED: "Hook that spells out the stakes" ("Iba 2º a 10 metros de la meta...", #2: 8 views). Try curiosity-gap hooks instead.
- TESTING: Irony arc (brag or confidence, then instant failure) is the strongest format. Evidence so far: #7.
- TESTING: Shorter clips (15–25 s) do better. Every clip so far was 22–43 s, so length shows no signal yet.
- TESTING: Series/prequel clips. #8 (prequel) got 19 vs. 8 for #2, but that's too few to call.
- No signal yet: music mood, clip length within 22–43 s.

## Prediction calibration
- Keep predicted viral chance between 25 and 60% until a clip passes 100 views.
- Next to each prediction, name the hypothesis the clip tests.
